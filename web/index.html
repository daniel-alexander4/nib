Welcome to Nib

Nib protects sensitive information — your signature images, signing identity, and saved files — in an encrypted vault on this computer.

An SSH key is required to lock and unlock that vault. It's a small key file on your machine, so there's no password to remember. You can use a key you already have, or let Nib create one for you.

That key is the only way in. Keep it safe and back it up — if you lose every authorized key, the vault (and your signatures) can't be recovered.
